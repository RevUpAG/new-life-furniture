About New Life Furniture

A Miami upholstery and fabrication workshop serving homes and businesses across South Florida for more than 30 years.

Craftsmanship you can see and feel

At New Life Furniture Mfg., we specialize in custom upholstered furniture, reupholstery, and fabrication. Our team combines decades of hands-on experience with quality materials to create pieces tailored to each client's vision.

Our work spans custom upholstered furniture, reupholstery, fine fabrics, leather, vinyl, marine upholstery, and window treatments including blinds, shades, and custom draperies. We handle both contract work and fine residential projects.

We proudly serve restaurants, hotels, hospitals, medical offices, retirement communities, and universities, along with homeowners, designers, and contractors.

How it works

A simple, transparent process

  1. 01

    Tell us about the piece

    Call, send the form, or share photos. We'll ask about size, use, and the look you want.

  2. 02

    Consultation & fabric

    We review the furniture and help you choose fabric, leather, vinyl, or marine-grade material for how it will be used.

  3. 03

    Clear written estimate

    You receive a free estimate with scope and timing before any work begins.

  4. 04

    Crafted in our Miami shop

    Our craftsmen rebuild, re-pad, and upholster by hand, then deliver the finished piece.
