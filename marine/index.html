Marine Upholstery

Comfortable, durable seating on the water

We replace cracked, faded, or waterlogged upholstery with materials chosen to resist UV, mildew, and moisture. Worn foam can be replaced at the same time so seats look good and feel supportive.

What's included

  • Helm and captain's chairs
  • Cockpit and bow cushions
  • Interior salon and cabin seating
  • Benches and sun pads
  • Foam replacement
  • Matching interior and exterior pieces

FAQ

Common questions

What materials do you use for marine upholstery?

We use marine-grade vinyl and outdoor performance fabrics designed to handle sun, salt, and moisture.

Can you replace the foam too?

Yes. Worn or waterlogged foam can be replaced while the cushion is being recovered.

How do I get an estimate?

Send photos of the seats or cushions, approximate sizes, and your boat's location using the form, or call us.
