How it works

A simple, transparent process

  1. 01

    Tell us about the piece

    Call, send the form, or share photos. We'll ask about size, use, and the look you want.

  2. 02

    Consultation & fabric

    We review the furniture and help you choose fabric, leather, vinyl, or marine-grade material for how it will be used.

  3. 03

    Clear written estimate

    You receive a free estimate with scope and timing before any work begins.

  4. 04

    Crafted in our Miami shop

    Our craftsmen rebuild, re-pad, and upholster by hand, then deliver the finished piece.

FAQ

Common questions

What types of businesses do you work with?

Restaurants, hotels, hospitals and medical offices, retirement communities, universities, and offices, along with the designers and contractors who serve them.

Can you work around our business hours?

Tell us your schedule when you request an estimate and we'll plan the work to limit disruption.

Which materials work best for commercial seating?

It depends on use. We'll recommend contract-grade vinyl, leather, or performance fabrics based on traffic, cleaning needs, and your design.

Do you handle large or multi-location projects?

Contact us with the scope and we'll discuss capacity and timing for your project.
