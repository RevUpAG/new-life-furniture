Furniture Reupholstery

Better than new, often for less than replacement

Well-built furniture is worth saving. Frames from decades ago are often stronger than what's sold today. Our upholsterers strip the piece to the frame, repair joints and springs, replace worn foam and padding, and recover it in the material you choose.

Not sure about fabric? We'll bring samples and guide you toward options that suit the room, the way you live, and South Florida's sun and humidity.

Wood refinishing, too

Exposed wood arms, legs, and dining tables can be refinished to match, so the whole piece looks complete.

What's included

  • Sofas, loveseats, and sectionals
  • Dining and accent chairs
  • Headboards and ottomans
  • Antique and heirloom pieces
  • New foam, padding, and spring repair
  • Wood touch-up and refinishing

FAQ

Common questions

Is it worth reupholstering my furniture instead of buying new?

If the frame is solid hardwood and you like the shape and size, reupholstery usually gives you a better-built piece than a comparable new one. We'll tell you honestly if a piece isn't worth the investment.

How much does reupholstery cost?

Cost depends on the size and style of the piece, the amount of fabric needed, the material you choose, and any frame or cushion repairs. Send us photos for a free estimate.

Can I supply my own fabric?

Yes. We'll confirm the yardage you need before you order. We can also help you select fabric, leather, or vinyl.

How long does it take?

Timing depends on the piece and material availability. Your written estimate includes the expected timeline.

Do you pick up and deliver?

Ask us when you request your estimate and we'll confirm the options for your location.
